## Featured: Ralven

> **Diagnose, maintain, and optimize Windows without guessing.**
> Ralven plans, validates, applies, verifies, and records every change it makes to your system, and reverses it where applicable.

- **Local diagnostics** for CPU, GPU, memory, storage, networking, power, drivers, startup, and Windows protections.
- **Light, Medium, and Aggressive profiles** built from known actions, with verification and rollback.
- **Apps and Games** from trusted sources to install, update, or remove, plus a hub of specialized tools.

[![Get it on the Microsoft Store](https://img.shields.io/badge/Get%20Ralven-Microsoft%20Store-0078D4?style=for-the-badge&logo=microsoft&logoColor=white)](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9N61X5M295M5) [![Learn more](https://img.shields.io/badge/Learn%20more-vemryx.com%2Fralven-000000?style=for-the-badge)](https://vemryx.com/ralven/)

## How we build

<table>
  <tr>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">
      <h4>Clarity over complexity</h4>
      Every feature has a stated purpose, and every important action is easy to understand before you commit to it.
    </td>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">
      <h4>Reliability by design</h4>
      We validate behavior, handle failure, and make changes reversible wherever we can.
    </td>
  </tr>
