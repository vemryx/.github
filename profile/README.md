Built, not just envisioned.
Vemryx is a Brazilian technology company developing its own software products while exploring new possibilities in artificial intelligence and aviation systems.
Our approach is straightforward: solve real problems, explain what the software does, and keep improving what we ship. From Windows utilities to intelligent flight simulation, each product has a clear purpose and its own engineering challenges.

How we approach engineering
Principle	What it means in practice
Clarity over complexity	Explain the purpose of every feature and make important actions understandable.
Reliability by design	Validate behavior, handle failures, and make changes reversible wherever possible.
Privacy and control	Give people meaningful choices about data, automation, and their systems.
Continuous ownership	Shipping is a milestone, not the end of maintaining a product.
